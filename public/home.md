# Look Checker

Look Checker offers free, browser-based tools for exploring visible facial proportions and personal style. Photos selected for scans are processed on the visitor's device and are not uploaded.

Results are estimates from a single image. Lighting, pose, camera distance, and image quality can affect them. They are not medical advice, an objective assessment of beauty, or a judgment of personal worth.
