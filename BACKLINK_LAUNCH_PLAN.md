# Look Checker: launch and link outreach plan

Prepared 20 October 2026. This plan is for the local project and is ready to use once the site has a stable public URL. Replace `[PUBLIC_URL]` with that URL before submitting anything.

## Safety and selection rules

- Submit to real communities or directories because their audiences may find the product useful, not just to obtain ranking credit.
- Do not buy dofollow links, automated submissions, guest posts that promise ranking credit, or packages of bulk backlinks.
- If a placement is paid advertising, ask the publisher to mark the link `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`. Do not pay for a dofollow link.
- Never pay for votes, reviews, or launch engagement.
- Do not list the app as medical, diagnostic, clinically validated, or AI-powered beyond what the product actually does.
- Do not submit while the site is only on localhost. Confirm that the public homepage and key pages load without login and that the About, Privacy, Contact, and tool pages work.

## Priority 1: Product Hunt

**Fit:** Strong as a product discovery launch; the main value is community exposure and feedback, not a guaranteed SEO backlink.

**Cost:** Free to post. Do not pay a third party to hunt, send traffic, or obtain votes.

**Submission:** [Product Hunt posting guide](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product). It requires a personal account and a working product URL. New accounts need onboarding; Product Hunt currently says new accounts wait one week before posting access. Post the product yourself and participate genuinely.

**Draft listing**

- Product: Look Checker
- Tagline: Free, private facial proportion and personal-style tools
- Description: Explore face shape, symmetry, and facial proportions with free tools that run in your browser. Photos stay on your device; readings are estimates from one image, not beauty ratings or medical judgments.
- Price: Free
- Primary URL: [PUBLIC_URL]
- Topics: Choose the closest currently available topics for beauty, lifestyle, or personal tools. Do not add unrelated AI or health topics just for reach.
- Gallery: Use actual product screenshots showing the tool, a sample result, and the privacy explanation. Do not show a real person's result without their permission.
- First comment: “Hi, I made Look Checker to let people explore face proportions and personal-style ideas without uploading a photo. The tools run in the browser; results are estimates from one image and aren't beauty ratings or medical advice. I'd value feedback on whether the explanations and limitations are clear.”

Create a draft first. Schedule the launch only after the public site is stable and you can answer questions. Product Hunt says products should be usable and the website should be clear; do not solicit votes.

## Priority 2: AlternativeTo

**Fit:** Reasonable for discovery as a free web application. Approval and timing are not guaranteed.

**Cost:** A free account is required to suggest an app. The site's FAQ says the normal review queue is very long and cannot be predicted. A paid priority review buys review timing only, not approval or ranking; no payment is recommended for this project.

**Submission:** [AlternativeTo FAQ: add a new application](https://alternativeto.net/faq/). Use “Suggest new application,” verify the account email, and wait for review. Do not promote the product through a user profile.

**Draft fields**

- Name: Look Checker
- Platform: Web
- Price/license: Free to use; do not describe it as open source.
- Short description: Free browser-based tools for exploring facial proportions and personal style. Photo scans run locally in the browser.
- Tags: Face shape, facial symmetry, personal style, browser-based tool
- Official website: [PUBLIC_URL]

Only connect it as an alternative to existing software if the products serve the same user need. Do not add it to unrelated apps to manufacture links.

## Priority 3: 10015.io Product Finder

**Fit:** Lower-priority product-directory exposure; the audience is mostly makers and developers, so it may send limited relevant visitors.

**Cost/link terms:** The current submission page says free listings get a nofollow link; optional paid features get a dofollow link. Use only the free listing. Skip paid features that sell ranking credit.

**Submission:** [Submit to 10015.io](https://www.10015.io/product-finder/submit). The site says live websites are required and submissions are manually reviewed; it currently estimates a 3–4 month free queue.

**Draft description:** Look Checker is a free web app for exploring face shape, symmetry, and facial proportions. Photo analysis runs in your browser; readings describe one image and are not beauty or medical judgments.

## Editorial mentions worth pursuing

Prioritize a genuine article, resource list, or review from a stylist, eyewear educator, photography educator, or privacy-focused technology writer whose audience would benefit. Offer a useful angle such as how camera distance changes a face photo or why a face-shape label should be treated as a styling prompt. Personalize each pitch and link to a specific useful guide. Do not demand a backlink, exact-match anchor text, or followed link.

**Pitch template**

Subject: A practical resource on face photos and proportion tools

Hi [Name],

I read your piece on [specific article]. One detail readers could also find useful is how a close phone camera, head angle, and lighting can change the proportions visible in a portrait.

I maintain Look Checker, a free browser-based set of face-shape and proportion tools. The photo analysis runs locally in the browser, and the site explains the limits of a single-image reading. This guide may be useful as a supporting resource: [RELEVANT_GUIDE_URL].

If it fits your editorial standards, feel free to review or reference it. No link placement or particular wording is expected. If anything in the guide is unclear or inaccurate, I welcome the correction.

Best,
[Your name]

Send only after inserting a real name and a personally relevant article. Do not mass-email the same message.
